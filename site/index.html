Diagrams as code for ServiceNow CSDM

Service models, drawn from text.

Write down your applications, instances, offerings and services. Say how they relate. csdmflow places every box, labels every line and colours every domain, the CSDM way.

Open the editor ↓ Read the docs
The one-minute tour

Stop pushing boxes around in slide decks.

Every change to a hand-drawn service model means re-placing boxes and re-routing lines. With csdmflow you edit two lines of text and the diagram redraws itself.

  • BeforePowerPoint, Visio or draw.io: hours per diagram, and again at every change.
  • AfterType the model, get it laid out the CSDM way.
  • ExportReal, editable shapes in PowerPoint and draw.io, lines glued to boxes.

The editor

Type a model and the drawing follows. Start from any of the examples, including every transcribed reference slide.

Browsers can't put editable PowerPoint shapes on the clipboard. Use Download as › PowerPoint and copy the diagram from that file.

One model, four outputs

            
Every format comes from the same layout, so a box sits in the same place whether you paste it into a deck or a wiki.

          

        

        

          

            .svg
            
SVG

            
Crisp at any size. Drops into docs, wikis and Confluence pages.

            
          

          

            .png
            
PNG

            
A 2× picture for chat, email and anywhere vectors aren't welcome.

            
          

          

            .pptx
            
PowerPoint

            
Real shapes, not a picture: each box holds its own text, every line keeps its arrow and dash.

            
          

          

            .drawio
            
draw.io

            
Download a file, or copy and paste shapes onto a canvas. Lines stay glued when you move a box.
